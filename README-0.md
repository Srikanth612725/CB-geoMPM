## Plane-strain and applicability notes
- The current charts/surrogate models assume 2D plane-strain response, which is conservative for mats with finite length because out-of-plane deformation is suppressed.
- Finite-length mats can develop 3D effects such as end bearing and edge punching; these can increase capacity relative to the plane-strain idealization and may require correction factors.
- Use the charts/surrogates as-is when the mat length is much larger than its width (practical strip-footing behavior). For shorter mats, consider applying 3D reduction factors or plan future validation against fully 3D analyses or tests.
